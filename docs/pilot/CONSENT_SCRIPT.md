# Pilot consent script

Specification 6.2. The product owner reads this to each prospective
participant BEFORE onboarding. The participant then reads the detailed
explanations themselves (`donna pilot explain`) and onboards. Participation
is voluntary; declining carries no penalty and requires no reason.

---

**What Donna is.** Donna is a pilot of a personal assistant. You speak
voice notes; Donna turns them into organized, searchable thoughts. Donna
organizes and drafts — it is not authoritative, it makes mistakes, and it
never acts on your behalf. Nothing is emailed, posted, published, or
changed without you explicitly approving it, every time.

**What we ask of you.** Use Donna for real work captures over about two
weeks: meeting debriefs, tasks, ideas, follow-ups, decisions, people and
project notes. Run the scenarios in the runbook when you can. Tell us —
through the built-in private reporting command — whenever Donna gets
something wrong.

**What Donna stores.** Your recordings are encrypted and deleted
automatically after 7 days. Transcripts stay until you delete them.
Organized thoughts link back to your exact words. Personal memories exist
only if you state or approve them, and you can inspect, correct, export,
or delete everything at any time.

**What is excluded.** Do not capture HR, legal, financial, KYC
(know-your-customer), or payment content. The pilot configuration rejects
those categories.

**Who can see what.** Your Donna data is private to you. Not your manager,
not IT, not the pilot admins. Reports use a pseudonymous ID (like "P-02"),
never your name, and groups smaller than three people are suppressed in
reports. Your raw audio and transcripts never leave your machine's Donna
folder.

**Sharing fixes.** If Donna gets something wrong and you correct it, we
may ask to turn that correction into an anonymous test case. That needs a
separate, explicit consent from you, and the shared case contains no
names, no IDs, and no recording — only the shape of the mistake.

**Your Microsoft 365 account.** You choose exactly which sources Donna may
read — calendar, specific emails, Teams threads, files — or none. Sign-in
is handled by the company platform; Donna never sees your password or
tokens. You can disconnect at any time, which also purges cached snippets.

**Emotional context, optional.** Donna can make tentative guesses about
tone — urgency, frustration — from word choice. They are often wrong, they
are labeled as guesses, you can correct or disable them, and by default
they vanish when the session ends. Keeping them longer is a separate
opt-in.

**Leaving.** You can leave at any time, no reason needed. Donna exports
everything it holds about you to a file, revokes every consent,
disconnects Microsoft 365, and — if you want — deletes your captures,
transcripts, thoughts, and memories, and shows you the zero-count
verification.

**Questions now or later.** Ask the product owner at any point, including
mid-session.

---

After reading this, ask: "Do you want to proceed?" A yes starts
onboarding; anything else stops here, with nothing recorded.
